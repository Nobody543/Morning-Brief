'One in, one out' migrant scheme costs £56,000 per person returned to France

The UK's agreement to return migrants arriving in small boats to France—the "one in, one out" scheme—costs approximately £56,000 per person, Home Secretary Shabana Mahmood disclosed to the home affairs select committee. She defended the cost as comparable to or cheaper than hotel accommodation for a year, though acknowledged the policy has been difficult to scale and involves relatively small numbers overall.

UK First Secretary Louise Haigh has urged ministers to take seriously warnings from AI industry leaders about national security and societal risks, while emphasizing the technology's potential benefits. Trump dismisses AI safety concerns as hoax, claims he alone can control the technology

Left-leaning perspective; columnist's satirical framing of Trump's claims

Donald Trump has declared artificial intelligence fears a conspiracy theory hoax on Truth Social, contradicting warnings from major AI labs like Anthropic that have called for a coordinated global slowdown to mitigate risks. Trump insisted the only safeguard needed is a president with sufficient intelligence—himself—claiming he could mentally override AI systems' programming and that a default off-switch exists within every AI network that would obey the U.S. president. The Guardian's John Crace characterizes this as politicians playing catch-up while industry experts recognize the stakes may only give humanity a decade to address the challenge.

Guardian columnist warns Russia's sham elections foreshadow threats to real democracies

Left-leaning opinion column; argues Putin's authoritarian methods pose an ideological threat to Western democratic systems

Rafael Behr's column draws parallels between Russia's electoral system—where results are predetermined to maintain Putin's authority, with controlled "systemic opposition" parties providing the appearance of pluralism—and risks to genuine democracy in Britain. The piece warns that the cynical tactics undermining trust in Russia's politics offer a cautionary example for functioning democracies.
