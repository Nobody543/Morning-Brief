UK government launches 'Be Ready' campaign urging public to stock emergency supplies

Defence Secretary Wes Streeting announced a government 'Be Ready' campaign focused on building national resilience, appearing on BBC Question Time alongside Conservative Tom Tugendhat to discuss the initiative with audiences concerned about everyday threats to national stability. The campaign has prompted measured increases in sales of essential supplies without triggering panic buying, according to shop owners stocking prepping materials—a marked contrast with the government's 1980 "Protect and Survive" nuclear war public information effort, which included advice on building fallout rooms and storing bodies and was met with public scepticism and ridicule.

Ukrainian forces use deception tactics against automated enemy surveillance in mass autonomous warfare

A commentary in War on the Rocks by Édouard R. Maurin—who served in the Pokrovsk command post in January 2025—describes how Ukrainian forces employ deception as a core tactical element in the increasingly automated Russo-Ukrainian conflict. Maurin recounts making a fake radio call he knew Russian forces would intercept; the intercepted message prompted Russian soldiers to reveal their position and prisoner, exposing their true situation.

He argues that on a battlefield saturated with sensors and drones, traditional concealment is insufficient—forces must instead generate false narratives (decoys, fake maneuvers, misleading intelligence) to induce wrong enemy assumptions and create "informational fog." The challenge is that modern targeting chains combine both human commanders and automated classifiers, so deception tactics must fool both. Maurin defines effective deception as forcing adversaries to spend more resources engaging decoys than the cost of creating them, while buying real maneuver freedom. He notes that mass autonomous warfare, dominated by unmanned aerial assets, has fundamentally reshaped tactical doctrine and makes diversion essential against drone interdiction on open terrain.
