Reform UK suspends two senior officials after undercover donations investigation

An undercover investigation by Channel 4 News, conducted by the group Verbatim posing as a potential donor and his wealthy American father, exposed Reform UK officials discussing ways to circumvent electoral laws on foreign donations. The scheme allegedly involved a foreign donor paying £30,000 for polling that benefited the party. James Orr, head of policy, and Dan Jukes, senior aide to Nigel Farage, stepped down following the broadcast. The suspensions came hours before Farage's scheduled conference speech, and Labour reported the party to police. The Electoral Commission stated it is in touch with the Met Police and reminded parties they are responsible for ensuring their own donor vetting processes are adequate.
