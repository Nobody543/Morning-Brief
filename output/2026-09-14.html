Government to debate changes to party donation rules following Reform's £372m fundraising haul

BBC reports factually; Guardian emphasizes political tensions and implications

The government is preparing to change rules on donations from British expats to UK political parties, following Reform UK's receipt of £372m in donations from crypto billionaires Christopher Harborne and Ben Delo over the past weekend. Housing Secretary Angela Rayner confirmed that any new rules would apply retrospectively, with anyone receiving donations outside the new rules required to return the money—though Reform insists its donations are within current law. Trade unions, MPs and donors have pressed Prime Minister Andy Burnham to add stronger curbs on UK-based donations, but Burnham has so far declined to insert limits on domestic donors into the elections bill. The BBC reports factually on the developments, while The Guardian provides more emphasis on the political tensions and fundraising implications. The donations represent a massive injection of resources into Reform's electoral machinery, raising questions about how such sums will be spent and whether the episode will catalyze broader political finance reform.
