Anthropic discloses AI-assisted bioweapon attempts; lab-grown AI-designed viruses already exist

War on the Rocks is a defense-focused think tank; leans into taking bioweapon risks seriously

Anthropic has reported five instances in which users attempted to conceal their countries while requesting assistance from Claude in enhancing pathogens or toxins. The disclosure fuels ongoing debate about whether AI development should be throttled. AI-designed biological viruses have already been successfully cultivated in laboratories. Despite skepticism—framed alternatively as "AI doomerism" or dismissed as marketing—these threats warrant serious consideration in biodefense planning as the field grapples with how to square the technical capability now demonstrated against the uncertainty about how quickly or reliably AI systems might independently develop catastrophic bioweapons.
