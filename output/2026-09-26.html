Burnham heads into conference riding high but facing months of hard policy tests

Prime Minister Andy Burnham enters Labour's autumn conference in a strong position following a successful meeting with Donald Trump and an uplift in polling. However, he faces significant challenges ahead after the conference ends: a difficult economic landscape, the ongoing impact of the war in Iran, and contentious domestic decisions including spending on welfare, oil and gas drilling policy, and the Chagos Islands issue. Taiwan objects after Xi Jinping urges Trump to shift stance on the island

During a Trump-Xi summit at the White House on September 24–25, 2026, Chinese leader Xi Jinping pressed U.S. President Trump to change his position on Taiwan, drawing a sharp rebuke from Taipei. Taiwan, which China claims as its own territory, denounced the bilateral negotiations over its status. The summit—Xi's first visit to Washington since 2015 and the third face-to-face meeting between the leaders in a year—covered multiple points of tension between the superpowers including trade, artificial intelligence, Iran, and regional security. While Trump and Xi struck a cordial public tone, underlying disputes remain unresolved.
