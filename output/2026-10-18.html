Yvette Cooper, the health secretary, announced £140m in funding for urgent care in England, citing preparations begun in July to manage an unusually challenging winter. Government Plans to Increase Apprenticeship Training Costs for Workers Over 25

The UK government is considering overhauling its apprenticeship funding scheme to make employers pay 25% more to train workers over 25 years old, compared to younger apprentices. The move is designed to tackle what ministers view as a youth jobs crisis by creating financial incentives for businesses to hire and train younger people rather than older workers entering training programs.

British Man Faces Deportation From Sweden Over Post-Brexit Residency Confusion

Mark Smith, a 60-year-old British national with severe health conditions including heart disease and stroke history, has gone into hiding in Sweden after being told he must leave the country by October 17 as an "alien" without legal residency. Smith claims he was informed three times by the government that no action was required for him to remain in Sweden after Brexit, but later received notice to depart—a situation he attributes to a government error in processing post-Brexit residency rights. The case highlights potential bureaucratic failures in how Brexit withdrawal agreements have been administered for British citizens in EU member states.
